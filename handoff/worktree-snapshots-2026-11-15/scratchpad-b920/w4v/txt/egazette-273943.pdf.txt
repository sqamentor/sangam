MINISTRY OF COMMERCE AND INDUSTRY 
(Department For Promotion Of Industry And Internal Trade) 
ORDER 
New Delhi, the 30th June, 2026 
S.O. 3503(E).— In exercise of the powers conferred by section 16 read with sub-section (3) of section 25 of 
the Bureau of Indian Standards Act, 2016 (11 of 2016), the Central Government, is of the opinion that it is necessary or 
expedient so to do in the public interest and after consulting the Bureau, hereby makes the following Order further to 
amend the Copper Products (Quality Control) Order, 2024, namely:- 
1. Short title and commencement. - (1) This Order may be called the Copper Products (Quality Control) Amendment 
Order, 2026. 
(2) It shall come into force on the date of its publication in the Official Gazette.  
2. In the Copper Products (Quality Control) Order, 2024, in paragraph 2, for third proviso, the following shall be 
substituted, namely: - 
            “Provided also that this order shall not apply from commencement of the Copper Products (Quality Control) 
Amendment Order, 2026 for a period up to the 30th November, 2026 to goods or articles imported by a manufacturer 
for the purpose of manufacturing equipment or product or sub-assemblies or component for Air Conditioning and 
Refrigeration in India as per the following TABLE, upto the volume of goods or articles specified under columns (2) of 
the following Table, namely:-
