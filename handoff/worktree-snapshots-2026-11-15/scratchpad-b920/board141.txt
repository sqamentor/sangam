@@TS@@ | W4-A/B61 | CHANGE | Decision 141 (RM-03). Old: BillingService.collect on a channel in sangam.billing.trusted-channels went through PaymentConfirmationPolicy.UnverifiedPaymentGate (actor from the request) and allocated at once. New: collect on a trusted channel is refused on every path (REST connector, packs, jobs); unverified acceptance exists only as the new plain-Java application service UnverifiedPayments (sangam-billing): accept, approve (maker-checker), resolve (VERIFIED, REJECTED, REVERSED), reconcile against the rail, ageing; the caller comes only from the server-authenticated identity (UnverifiedPaymentAuthority.Caller built by the REST adapter from RequestContext), never from a payload or a pack; state PAYMENT_ACCEPTED_UNVERIFIED is pending and posts nothing to the ledger until VERIFIED; transitions are guarded by state and version under a row lock with the authority rechecked inside; unique (tenant, channel, payment_ref). PaymentConfirmationPolicy.UnverifiedPaymentGate and acceptUnverified are removed, fromConfiguration loses its gate argument. NEW table BILL_UNVERIFIED_PAYMENT in sangam-ledger LedgerSchema (the billing tables, generator input). NEW per-channel keys sangam.billing.unverified.channels.<channel>.{policy-version,max-amount,currency,maker-checker,ageing-alert-after}: any missing means that channel is disabled. NEW REST /api/v1/billing/{ledger}/unverified-payments (UnverifiedPaymentController). | W4-B, W4-G, W4-M, integrator
