# USA healthcare workflows and regulatory controls (US-HEALTH workstream)

Status: 2026-11-05. Scope: the US healthcare workflows that Sangam must run end to end, the regulatory controls each one enforces, and the interoperability standards its connectors must speak. Requirements are numbered **US-HLT-nnn**. ADR-D-23 names USA health (HIPAA, HL7 FHIR R4, X12 837/835, CMS-0057-F) as one of the first jurisdiction packs. Under ADR-D-20 none of this is hard-coded: every timeline is in a DMN table that a tenant can override (GP-37), and every external system is reached through a connector.

Implementation:
- `sangam/sangam-connectors-health` (`org.sangam.connectors.health`): generic FHIR R4 client (REST, SMART Backend Services, search, transaction, Subscriptions → inbox), X12 005010 encoder and decoder (270/271, 276/277, 278, 837P/837I, 835, 999/TA1), Da Vinci PAS flow, CDS Hooks service and client, NPI validation. Each one has a simulator profile.
- `sangam/sangam-packs`: 8 packs `sec.ushealth.*`, generated by `tools/packgen/sec_ushealth.py`, with English and Spanish (`es`) labels.

How to read the sources:
- **Primary**: the regulation or the standard itself (eCFR, Federal Register, HL7, X12, CMS, HHS, ONC/ASTP).
- The CMS-0057-F figures were checked on the CMS fact sheet on 2026-11-05. The eCFR pages refused automated fetching (bot gate), so the CFR section numbers and time limits below come from the regulation text as published. They are marked *(verify)* where a 2024–2026 amendment or a court ruling may have changed them. Every such value is in DMN.
- **SYNTHETIC** marks a pilot value (for example a payer policy) that the tenant replaces.

## 17. Verify before go-live

1. 45 CFR 164.524 time limit: the 2020 NPRM proposed 15 + 15 days; not finalised as of this note. The DMN `sec-ushealth-access-clocks` holds 30D / 30D.
2. 45 CFR 164.509 (reproductive health): vacated in 2025; DMN switch off.
3. HIPAA Security Rule 2025 NPRM: not final.
4. CMS-0057-F 1-business-day Provider Access / Payer-to-Payer response time: confirm in the rule text.
5. PPDR administrative fee and the $400 threshold: confirm the current CMS amounts.
6. State breach-notification laws shorter than 60 days: add tenant overrides of `sec-ushealth-breach-clocks`.
7. Payer-specific PA rules (QHP on FFE, commercial plans) differ from CMS-0057-F: `sec-ushealth-pa-clocks` has rows by `payerProgram`.
