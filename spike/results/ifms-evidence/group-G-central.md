| SNA-SPARSH mechanics | (i) The central Ministry opens a drawing account with RBI under its UDCH code. (ii) The State designates an SNA per **State Linked Scheme (SLS)**; an SLS must have a uniform Centre:State ratio, and umbrella schemes are split into separate SLSs by ratio. (iii) The State opens **SLS-wise drawing accounts at RBI**. (iv) Schemes are marked on PFMS and CSS are mapped to SLSs. (v) Old SNA bank accounts are closed and unspent balances returned to the CFI and the State CF. (vii) A **Mother Sanction** per State per CSS sets the drawing limit and may be modified with IFD concurrence. (viii) SNAs and IAs are **registered in the State IFMIS**. (ix) IAs generate payment files in the State IFMIS, and the treasury consolidates them. (x) States without IT readiness may route manual claims through the treasury. (xi) The State builds a **Cyber Treasury** that flags the SNA/IA and the SLS on each file. (xii-xiii) The consolidated file goes to PFMS, the Ministry generates a central-share sanction (**child sanction**) and pre-funds the State's RBI drawing account, and the Mother Sanction is reduced. (xiv) **3 PM cut-off**: files received by then are sanctioned the same working day. (xv) The State IFMIS auto-pushes the file to RBI, RBI debits and pays, and the debit notification goes to both PFMS and the State IFMIS. (xvi) A **master of Centre/State/top-up proportions** is kept in both the State IFMIS and PFMS, and the top-up must be shown separately. (xvii) Failed transactions are re-pushed with a **reissue flag** to prevent duplicates. (xviii) No diversion to PD accounts. (xix) UTs without legislature work directly in PFMS; UTs with legislature may get an LoA. | same | yes | V |

## Candidate cross-state requirements

- **IFS-001** Configurable chart of accounts in six tiers below the LMMH sector and sub-sector: MH (4-digit), SMH (2), MinH (3), Sub-Head, Detailed Head, Object Head. The widths of the state-owned tiers are set per state profile.
- **IFS-002** MH, SMH and MinH are treated as a **centrally governed reference dataset** from CGA's LMMH. States cannot create codes at these tiers. Each code has a validity period and a link to the correction slip that changed it (e.g. CS 1128-1132, MH 2246/4245).
- **IFS-003** A correction-slip ingestion workflow: draft, review, effective-date activation, and a remap or merge of affected budget lines. The audit trail keeps the CS number.
- **IFS-004** MH first-digit semantics are enforced: 0/1 receipt, 2/3 revenue expenditure, 4/5 capital, 6/7 loans, 8 Public Account. Validation stops postings to the wrong section.
- **IFS-005** Three-part fund model (Consolidated Fund, Contingency Fund, Public Account), with sector and sub-sector rollups.
- **IFS-006** A charged/voted attribute on every expenditure budget line and transaction, carried through to Appropriation Accounts.
- **IFS-007** Standard Minor Heads (001, 800, 911 and others, plus the new 503) are available as a reusable template under any MH.
- **IFS-008** A state-defined object-head master, mapped to IGAS-2 categories, including the "GIA for creation of capital assets" flag.
- **IFS-009** Separate mappings between the budget (Demands for Grants and units of appropriation) and the accounts head structure.
- **IFS-010** IGAS-1 guarantee register, IGAS-3 loans-and-advances register, and IGAS-2 grant classification, each with the statement formats used in the Finance Accounts.
- **IFS-011** A Scheme master in which each CSS is decomposed into **SLSs, each with exactly one Centre:State ratio** and an optional **top-up** component. The ratio is versioned and effective-dated.
- **IFS-012** Agency registry for SNAs and IAs at any depth, mapped to the SLS and synchronised with the PFMS agency codes.
- **IFS-013** **Mother Sanction** fetch from PFMS: one active sanction per SLS, an available-limit check, and **auto-lapse at 23:59 on 31 March**. Payments are blocked when no Mother Sanction exists.
- **IFS-014** Payment-file generation by IAs, then treasury scrutiny, then a **Cyber Treasury consolidated file** carrying SNA/IA and SLS flags. Central, state and top-up shares are split explicitly.
- **IFS-015** Configurable submission cut-off to PFMS (default **15:00**). Next-working-day logic follows a holiday calendar that includes RBI e-Kuber "global holidays".
- **IFS-016** Child (central-share) sanction receipt from PFMS, pre-funding of the State RBI drawing account, then an **auto-push to RBI e-Kuber**. RBI debit and failure notifications are ingested.
- **IFS-017** Failed-transaction handling: partial failure, rectification, then a re-push with a mandatory **reissue tag** that blocks duplicate payment.
- **IFS-018** SLS-wise reconciliation across the State IFMIS, PFMS and e-Kuber, with the treasury as the sole reconciliation unit. AG account submission is generated.
- **IFS-019** No diversion of CSS funds to PD or bank accounts under SPARSH. A policy rule blocks such transfers.
- **IFS-020** Legacy SNA (2021 model) support: an SCB Single Nodal Account, ZBSAs or drawing limits for IAs, interest remittance to the CF, an annual undertaking, and closure with return of unspent balances on migration to SPARSH.
- **IFS-021** Deductions inside CSS payment files (TDS, IT, GST, employee contributions, EMD, SD) routed to the correct heads, per the CGA SOP of 2025.
- **IFS-022** DBT payment files carry the DBT Mission scheme code and an APBS or account-based flag. Beneficiary validation uses the NPCI mapper or account validation. Reports go to the State DBT portal and DBT Bharat in the prescribed integration format.
- **IFS-023** A PFMS integration-model selector per scheme or agency: Model 1 (external EAT/DBT), Model 2 (external system, payment via PFMS), Model 3 (PFMS UI), Model 4 (State IFMIS).
- **IFS-024** Daily treasury-to-PFMS data sharing on funds devolved to IAs, covering both the Centre and State contributions.
- **IFS-025** RBI e-Kuber connector for e-payments and e-receipts, with standard message formats, an e-scroll and DN/CN ingestion, and year-end special working-day handling (31 March circulars).
- **IFS-026** Agency-bank master: which bank handles which receipt or payment business, the date of State Finance Department approval, and RBI authorisation. Agency banks settle directly with RBI.
- **IFS-027** GRAS-style e-receipt module: GRN and e-challan, multi-bank and gateway payment, CIN return, bank e-scroll, RBI transaction-level data, then an automatic receipt voucher under the correct receipt head.
- **IFS-028** GST (SGST, IGST settlement) receipt reconciliation between GSTN data and RBI credits.
- **IFS-029** TSA-style assignment accounts at RBI for state autonomous bodies and agencies (optional, state policy), with a year-end lapse and zero opening balance.
- **IFS-030** Delegation-of-powers engine: authority by rank or office, monetary limit by object or transaction type, and a re-appropriation rule set. It is configurable to DFPR 2024 or to the state's own delegation rules, and effective-dated.
- **IFS-031** Procurement rule-pack hooks per state (GFR or state rules, Procurement Manuals 2024/2025, CPWD Works Manual, PPP-MII local-content classes and thresholds, MSE preference), versioned by edition.
- **IFS-032** Just-in-time release policy as a configurable control: no advance release beyond the need-based limit (GFR 230(7)).
- **IFS-033** Sub-state fund entities (Sixth Schedule ADC District or Regional Funds, ULBs, PRIs) as separate accounting entities, each with its own CAG-prescribed form of accounts and its own audit. They are funded by State grant-in-aid object heads and Art. 275(1) flows.
- **IFS-034** Art. 275(1), Scheduled Area and TSP fund tagging, so that ST-welfare grants can be traced from the Union through the State to the ADC or district.
- **IFS-035** Fund-sharing presets by state category (General 60:40 / 50:50; NE and Himalayan 90:10 / 80:20; UT without legislature 100 %; UT with legislature per scheme), each overridable per scheme guideline.
- **IFS-036** UT-without-legislature mode: operates directly in PFMS with no State RBI drawing account, or under an LoA (Letter of Authority) for UTs with legislature.
- **IFS-037** Transfers from the State IFMS to ULBs and RLBs routed through IFMS (CAG's recommendation to the 16th FC).

## Implications for a state-profile configuration model

1. **Two-layer chart of accounts.** A read-only **central layer**, LMMH (sector, MH, SMH, MinH, standard minor heads), is published as a versioned reference package that is updated per CGA correction slip. A writable **state layer** holds sub-head, detailed head and object head, with code widths, labels and budget mappings. A state profile chooses its coding widths and its separator or rendering format (e.g. `2215-80-188-01-01-31`).
2. **Effective dating everywhere.** LMMH slips, GFR updates, DFPR amendments, CSS ratios, SPARSH onboarding dates (01-07-2025, 01-11-2025) and procurement editions all change mid-year. Every rule pack needs `effective_from` and `effective_to` dates and a source citation.
3. **State category attributes** on the profile: `category` (General, NE, Himalayan, UT-with-legislature, UT-without-legislature), `rbi_banker` (yes, or limited for Sikkim), `ekuber_epayment` and `ekuber_ereceipt` flags, `sixth_schedule_areas[]` and `scheduled_areas`. These attributes drive the default sharing ratios, the treasury integration paths and the sub-state entities.
4. **Integration adapters as profile options.** The PFMS adapter (SNA-SPARSH: Mother Sanction, payment-file and child-sanction exchange, DN/CN, reconciliation), the RBI e-Kuber adapter, the GSTN adapter, the DBT Bharat and NPCI adapters, and the GRAS gateway are interface contracts shared by all states. The profile holds endpoints, cut-offs (15:00 PFMS, 18:00 bank luggage), holiday calendars and certificates. It does not hold the logic.
5. **Rule packs, not code.** Delegation of powers (DFPR 2024 or state DFPR), Treasury Code checks, procurement thresholds and PPP-MII classes are expressed as data-driven rule packs per state. Central packs (GFR, DFPR 2024) are templates that a state can fork.
6. **Entity hierarchy.** The profile defines the entity tree: Union, State (CF, Contingency Fund, Public Account), Treasury and Cyber Treasury, SNA/IA per SLS, and sub-state fund entities (ADC District Funds, ULBs, PRIs, autonomous bodies under TSA). Each node has its own account set, accounting form and audit authority.
7. **Reconciliation as a first-class workflow.** SPARSH makes the State Treasury the single reconciliation point across IFMS, PFMS, e-Kuber and the AG. The profile configures the reconciliation calendar, tolerances and AG submission formats.
8. **Open verification items.** Before freezing defaults, verify the [U] rows: the LMMH General Directions on tier ownership; the Union object-head list; IGAS-1 notification details; the 2024 CNA master circular thresholds; the 2026 DFPR amendment; the 2nd edition (2025) of the Works Manual; CPWD Works Manual 2024; and the current per-state e-Kuber list.
