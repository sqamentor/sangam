## Cross-state observations

- **SNA-SPARSH is live in all four** (WB, OD and SK verified; AS only via the generic CGA statement). Each state implements it differently: WB uses a "virtual limit to executing agencies, no DDO allotment" model plus a Cyber Treasury aggregator; Odisha runs CSS-JiT alongside its own **state JiT-FS** for state schemes; Sikkim created a separate **SNA-SPARSH Cyber Treasury** accounting unit. The platform needs one configurable JIT model, not one hard-coded flow.
- **Banking rail is not universal.** Three states pay via RBI e-Kuber (WB 100%; OD via CePC). **Sikkim has no RBI government business at all** and pays by cheque on the State Bank of Sikkim. The payment adapter must allow a non-RBI, cheque-based bank.
- **Treasury topology varies.** Units include out-of-state offices (OD and WB at New Delhi; AS Shillong South in Meghalaya), Cyber Treasuries (WB now 2, OD 1, SK 1), dedicated pension treasuries/offices (OD, WB) and non-banking CPAOs (SK). The topology needs a typed, data-driven treasury master.
- **Rulebooks are old and mid-revision.** WBTR 2005 has an approved revision pending Gazette and WBFR is "under revision"; Odisha's OGFR 2023 replaced older rules but DFPR dates from 1978; Assam's ATR 2017 was amended in 2023 and DFPR dates from 1999; SFR dates from 1979 with a 2024 amendment. Validation rules should be versioned with effective dates and citations.
- **HoA extensions differ.** WB has a 17-digit code with NP/SP and a sub-detailed head [U]; OD adds an object head and an A/B expenditure type; SK shows extra segments. Budget classification must be a pluggable segment schema.
- **Pre-bill approvals are first-class objects in all IFMSes.** Examples: WB AAFS, Assam AA/FS, Odisha's mandatory IFMS sanction order. Bills cannot exist without a sanction object.
- **Autonomous-body fund flow is a common need** (WB GTA via Home & Hill Affairs; Assam BTC/KAAC/DHAC with own fund rules; Odisha SBMS for ULBs/GPs). The model is grant pass-through with UC loops, often with central money routed via the state.
- **Language:** Odia/English/Hindi is mandated per service flow in the OD RFP; Assamese UI exists in AS; Bengali and Nepali are likely in WB/SK but unverified. i18n is a hard requirement, not optional.
- **Gaps to close later:** procurement and works codes (OPWD Code, WB e-tender rules, Assam procurement, Sikkim PWD manual); state DBT rails (KALIA, Orunodoi, Lakshmir Bhandar or its successor); Assam HoA format and current treasury count; Odisha ITDA/Fifth Schedule fund flow; Sikkim SIFMS module list.
